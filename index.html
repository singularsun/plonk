HOW TO PLAY

Knock your opponents off the arena! There are no attack buttons — damage and knockback come purely from ramming into other players with your ball.

Move: A/D (or arrow keys). Jump: W. Heavy: hold Space to increase your density — you sink faster and hit harder, but you're slower to steer.

Dash: M. Grapple (Grapple mode only): hold N to fire a rope at the nearest surface and swing.

Everyone has exactly one life. There's no time limit — the match runs until only one player is left.

Modes: Regular (plain arena, no grappling) and Grapple (swing on ropes).
